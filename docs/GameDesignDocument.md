## 1. Executive Summary

You wake on a shore that should not exist, holding a compass that points at nothing. The Realm broke a thousand years ago; what is left drifts as **Shards** — chunks of a dead world reassembled by a wounded machine called the Loom. You survive it, map it, tame the creatures the Loom stitched from its own memory, and decide whether to repair the Loom or let the Realm end cleanly.

**One-line pitch:** *Don't Starve's survival pressure, Pokémon's collection loop, and a world that is genuinely different every run — but your base, your creatures, and your story persist across all of it.*

### 1.1 Design Pillars

1. **The world is honest.** Procedural, but never arbitrary. Every biome edge, ruin, and resource cluster is generated by rules the player can learn and exploit. Mastery beats luck.
2. **Creatures are companions, not inventory.** A creature you have carried for 20 hours has a name you gave it, scars from bosses, and a bond stat that changes its AI. Losing one hurts.
3. **The base is the anchor.** Procedural exploration is centrifugal; the base pulls you home. Everything you build makes the next expedition further, safer, weirder.
4. **Mobile-first, not mobile-compromised.** One thumb plays the whole game. The desktop build is the same game with a bigger window, not a different one.
5. **Death is a story beat, not a reset.** Standard mode drops carried inventory and injures creatures. Hardcore mode exists for those who want it.

### 1.2 The Core Loop

```
        ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
        │                                                 │
   PREPARE ──► EXPLORE ──► ENCOUNTER ──► RESOLVE ──► RETURN
   (craft,     (procedural  (creature,   (combat,    (deposit,
    equip,      shards,      NPC, event,  tame,       build,
    party)      dungeons)    boss)        flee,       bond,
        ▲                                 barter)     evolve)
        │                                                 │
        └──────────── progression unlocks new prep ───────┘
```

**Micro loop (30–90s):** move → spot resource/threat → decide (gather / fight / tame / avoid) → act → resource or XP tick.
**Meso loop (10–25 min):** expedition out, hit a Shard objective, come home before the Duskveil rolls in.
**Macro loop (2–6 h):** unlock a new Shard tier, upgrade the Anchor, evolve a creature line, close an Act.

## 2. Story

### 2.1 Premise

The player is a **Wayfinder** — one of the few people whose memory survives passage through the Rift. You arrive with nothing but a **Loomcompass**, a broken instrument that only points toward things that are *wrong*.

### 2.2 Structure — Five Acts

**Act I — Shorebound** *(0–3 h, tutorial + first Shard)*
Wash up on the Verdant Reach. Meet **Elder Ossa**, a Keeper who has been maintaining a dead ritual for 400 years and cannot remember why. Build the first Anchor. Tame the first creature. Discover that the Loomcompass is drawing a map that predicts terrain you have not visited yet.

**Act II — The Cartographer's Debt** *(3–12 h)*
**Vessa Quill**, a Skysail smuggler, offers passage between Shards for a price you cannot pay in coin — she wants the compass. Establish trade, meet the four factions, unlock the Skiff. The first world boss, **The Chorus of Rust**, appears when the player first crosses a Shard boundary.

**Act III — The Wound** *(12–28 h)*
Descend into the Hollow Deep. Learn the truth: the Loom did not break. It was *sabotaged* by the last Wayfinder, **Cael**, to stop it from overwriting the Realm's inhabitants with a "corrected" version. Every creature the player has tamed is a Loom draft — a rough copy of something the Loom is still trying to finish. Bond levels are the creature resisting revision.

**Act IV — The Correction** *(28–45 h)*
The Loom accelerates. Shards begin overwriting each other; the Duskveil event system escalates from nightly nuisance to territorial invasion. Factions fracture over whether to help. The player must choose faction allegiance, which gates two of four endings.

**Act V — Thread's End** *(45 h+)*
Confrontation at the Loom Core. Four endings:

| Ending | Requirement | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Restore** | Repair Loom, 3+ faction rep at Trusted | Realm is rebuilt — but your creatures are "corrected" into their final forms and lose their bonds. Bittersweet. |
| **Sever** | Destroy the Loom, Cael's journals ×7 | Realm stops decaying but stops growing. World becomes finite; procedural generation locks. Your creatures stay yours. |
| **Weave** | Bond ≥ 8 on 10 creatures + Anchor tier 5 | You replace the Loom. Post-game: the player defines generation rules (a diegetic world-editor). *True ending.* |
| **Drift** | Simply leave via the Rift | 12-minute epilogue, credits, NG+ with a "Remembered" seed carrying creatures forward. |

### 2.3 Narrative Delivery

- **No cutscene longer than 40 seconds.** Mobile respects interruption.
- **Environmental first:** ruins, item flavor text, creature bestiary entries written by Cael.
- **Barks over dialogue trees** for ambient NPCs; full dialogue only for the 9 named characters.
- **The Bestiary is the novel.** Each of the 40 creatures has a 3-stage entry that unlocks at bond 1/5/9, and the stage-3 entries collectively spell out Cael's sabotage.

## 3. Lore

### 3.1 The Realm and the Loom

The Realm was never natural. It was **woven** — a constructed world maintained by the Loom, a distributed machine of ten thousand Threadstones buried in the crust. The Loom's mandate: *keep the pattern coherent.* For nine centuries it did so beautifully. Then it began correcting the inhabitants toward the pattern, not the pattern toward the inhabitants.

### 3.2 The Sundering (Year 0)

Cael, Wayfinder and Loomwright, drove a **Null Spindle** into the Core. The Loom did not die. It *stuttered*. The Realm fragmented into Shards, each drifting in the Between, each running an out-of-date copy of the pattern. This is the in-fiction justification for procedural generation: **the world regenerates because the Loom keeps re-deriving it from a corrupted seed.**

### 3.3 Factions

| Faction | Creed | Wants from player | Rep rewards |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Keepers of Ossa** | Preserve the pattern; the Loom is holy | Rituals restored, Threadstones returned | Healing, revival, blessing buffs, Restore ending |
| **Skysail Compact** | Trade across the Between; neutrality is profit | Rare goods, cartography data | Fast travel, market access, cargo capacity |
| **The Unwoven** | Free will over pattern; burn the Loom | Sabotage, Null shards, freed creatures | Wild-creature taming bonus, Sever ending, illegal tech |
| **Hollowborn** | Creatures of the Deep who achieved self-pattern | Understanding, bond mastery | Evolution catalysts, Weave ending, deep-creature access |

Reputation is **-100 to +100** in five bands: Hostile / Wary / Neutral / Trusted / Sworn. Helping one faction directly costs another at a 1:0.4 ratio (never 1:1 — the player should never feel locked out by a single act).

### 3.4 The Eight Threads (Elements)

| Thread | Domain | Color | Strong vs | Weak vs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Verdant** | Growth, poison, regrowth | #6BBF59 | Stone | Ember |
| **Stone** | Mass, armor, inertia | #A98D6B | Storm | Verdant |
| **Storm** | Speed, chain, disruption | #E2C044 | Tide | Stone |
| **Tide** | Flow, cold, erosion | #4A9FD4 | Ember | Storm |
| **Ember** | Heat, burst, decay | #E0603C | Verdant | Tide |
| **Radiance** | Order, healing, reveal | #F2E9C9 | Umbra | Umbra |
| **Umbra** | Entropy, drain, conceal | #6A4A9E | Radiance | Radiance |
| **Null** | Absence (endgame only) | #2A2A32 | All (×1.25) | Nothing; takes ×1.25 from all |

Five-cycle: `Verdant → Stone → Storm → Tide → Ember → Verdant`. Multiplier **×1.5** advantage, **×0.67** disadvantage. Radiance/Umbra are mutually ×1.5 (high-risk mirror). Dual-thread creatures multiply both.

*Why a 5-cycle plus a mirror pair instead of an 18-type chart:* it is memorizable in one screen, teachable on a phone, and produces the same tactical depth with 1/10th the balance surface.

## 4. World Design

### 4.1 Topology

The world is **not one continuous map**. It is a graph of **Shards** — finite procedural islands (typically 2048×2048 tiles ≈ 1.3 km² at 32px/tile) connected by **Riftgates**.

```
        [Verdant Reach]───[Ashen Wastes]
              │       ╲         │
              │        ╲        │
        [Sunken Mire]──[Dust Sea]───[Frostspire]
              ╲            │        ╱
               ╲           │       ╱
                ───[The Hollow Deep]───
                          │
                     [Loom Core]
```

Each Shard is generated from `hash(worldSeed, shardId)`. **Deterministic and permanent** — a Shard visited in hour 2 is identical in hour 40, including the tree the player chopped (persisted as a delta, see TechnicalDesign §5).

**Why a Shard graph and not one giant world:** it bounds memory (one Shard resident at a time), makes streaming trivial, gives the narrative a natural gating mechanism, and lets each biome own its ruleset instead of blending mush at the borders.

### 4.2 Biomes

Seven biomes, each with distinct generation rules, hazard, resource set, and creature pool.

| Biome | Danger | Signature hazard | Key resources | Creature pool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Verdant Reach** | ★☆☆☆☆ | None (safe tutorial) | Timber, fiber, berries, copper | Verdant, Stone |
| **Ashen Wastes** | ★★★☆☆ | Heat drain (needs Coolant or Frost gear); ash storms every ~9 min | Obsidian, sulfur, iron, emberglass | Ember, Stone |
| **Frostspire** | ★★★☆☆ | Cold drain; blizzard whiteout reduces view to 6 tiles | Rime crystal, silver, pelts | Tide, Radiance |
| **Sunken Mire** | ★★☆☆☆ | Toxic water (stacking Poison); no fast travel | Reeds, spore caps, bog iron, alchemy reagents | Verdant, Tide, Umbra |
| **Dust Sea** | ★★★★☆ | No water source; sandstorms bury structures | Glass, gold, relic fragments | Stone, Storm |
| **Hollow Deep** | ★★★★★ | Total darkness; Sanity drain without light | Threadstone, void ore, deep fungus | Umbra, Null |
| **Loom Core** | ★★★★★★ | Reality shear (random tile rewrites) | Loom alloy, pattern shards | Null, all |

Biome assignment uses a **Whittaker-style temperature × moisture lookup**, not random pick — so a Shard reads as a coherent place, with deserts near hot/dry and mires near hot/wet. See TechnicalDesign §8.

### 4.3 Points of Interest

Generated per Shard by Poisson-disk sampling with biome-weighted tables:

- **Anchor Sites** (1 per Shard) — buildable base plots
- **Ruins** (3–7) — hand-authored room templates, stitched procedurally
- **Dungeons** (1–3) — see §4.4
- **Riftgates** (2–4) — travel + save points
- **Wild Dens** (5–12) — creature spawners, higher-level than open-field
- **Caches** (8–20) — loot, some trapped
- **Wanderer Camps** (1–3) — NPC merchants/questgivers, migrate every 3 in-game days

### 4.4 Dungeons

Three archetypes, all procedurally assembled from hand-authored rooms:

| Archetype | Layout algorithm | Length | Reward tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warren** | Cellular-automata cave, organic | 15–25 rooms | Resources, common creatures |
| **Vault** | BSP grid + locked-door graph | 8–14 rooms | Gear, blueprints, mini-boss |
| **Spiral** | Ring corridors + radial spokes | 20–30 rooms | Boss, evolution catalyst, lore |

Every dungeon guarantees: one **key-lock loop** (find key → open gate → shortcut back), one **elite encounter**, one **choice room** (two doors, one reward each, take one).

### 4.5 Time, Weather, and the Duskveil

- Day = 20 real minutes (14 day / 6 night). Configurable in accessibility options.
- **Duskveil:** at night, Umbra-thread hostiles spawn in a spreading radius from Shard-specific corruption anchors. Intensity scales with Act. Building **Wardlights** at the base holds it back — this is the primary reason base building matters mechanically, not decoratively.
- Weather is per-Shard, seeded, and predictable one cycle ahead via the **Sky Chart** upgrade (rewards preparation, the game's core virtue).

## 5. Character Design

### 5.1 The Wayfinder (Player)

Fully customizable, non-gendered defaults, 40+ cosmetic options at start, no stat differences between appearances.

**Attributes** (5, each 1–50):

| Attr | Governs |
|---|---|
| **Vigor** | Max HP, carry weight |
| **Focus** | Max Stamina, crit chance, taming success |
| **Attunement** | Magic damage, creature bond gain rate, Sanity resist |
| **Craft** | Craft speed, quality roll, build cost reduction |
| **Grit** | Damage resist, status resist, hazard drain reduction |

+1 point per level (100 levels), +3 per Act milestone. Respec available at any Anchor for Threadsilver.

**Survival meters:** Health, Stamina, Hunger, Warmth (biome-dependent), Sanity (Deep/night only). Hunger and Warmth drain *slowly* — they exist to shape route planning, not to nag. Zero Hunger caps max HP at 50%, it does not kill.

## 6. Creatures

**40 collectible creatures across 16 evolution lines**, plus 8 bosses (§12).

### 6.1 Creature Stats

Each creature has: HP, ATK, DEF, MAG, RES, SPD (base 20–120), one or two Threads, a **Trait** (passive), and a **Nature** (stat bias ±10%, randomized on capture). Level 1–60. Stats grow via `stat = base + floor(base * level * 0.021) + IV(0..15)`.

**Bond (0–10)** is the signature system. It rises from co-combat, feeding preferred food, and carrying the creature through biome hazards. Bond affects:

| Bond | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bestiary entry 1; obeys basic commands |
| 3 | +5% all stats; will flee-guard the player at low HP |
| 5 | Bestiary entry 2; unlocks 4th skill slot |
| 7 | Can evolve; +10% all stats |
| 9 | Bestiary entry 3; unlocks **Thread Resonance** (player+creature combo attack) |
| 10 | Refuses Loom correction — required for Weave ending |

### 6.3 Roles

Six roles define AI archetype and party synergy: **Tank** (holds aggro, high DEF), **Bruiser** (sustained melee), **Assassin** (burst, low HP), **Skirmisher** (mobile, hit-and-run), **Mage** (ranged AoE), **Support/Healer** (buffs, cleanse). Party = 3 active + 6 reserve. A balanced party gets a **Formation bonus** (e.g., Tank+Mage+Support = +8% RES).

### 6.4 Taming

Taming is a **skill check, not a dice roll**, so it stays satisfying on the 200th creature:

1. Reduce target below 40% HP (or apply Sleep/Charm).
2. Throw a **Threadsnare** — opens a 3-beat rhythm mini-game (tap in the shrinking ring).
3. Success chance = `base(rarity) + hits*0.18 + Focus*0.006 + statusBonus − levelDelta*0.02`, clamped [0.05, 0.95].
4. Failure costs the snare, aggros the target, and grants +0.05 permanent affinity with that species (mercy mechanic).

Legendaries and bosses require a scripted multi-phase tame instead.

### 6.5 Evolution

Three gates: **level**, sometimes **bond**, sometimes a **catalyst item or condition**. Evolution is reversible once per creature via a **Devolution Draught** (so players can un-ruin a favorite's look — a real retention issue in this genre). Evolving grants +1 skill slot, a new Trait tier, and a stat rebase (not a reset — the creature keeps its IVs and bond).

## 7. Progression Systems

### 7.1 Progression Tracks (five parallel, deliberately)

| Track | Currency of advancement | Gates |
|---|---|---|
| **Wayfinder Level** | Combat/quest XP | Attributes, gear tiers |
| **Creature Roster** | Taming, bond, evolution | Party power, Thread coverage |
| **Anchor (base)** | Materials, blueprints | Storage, crafting tiers, Duskveil defense, fast travel |
| **Faction Reputation** | Quests, gifts, choices | Vendors, endings, unique blueprints |
| **Knowledge** | Discovery, bestiary, lore | Recipes, map reveal, hazard resistance |

Any one track can be pushed alone for ~4 hours before it hard-gates on another. That is deliberate: it prevents single-track grinding and keeps all five systems in rotation.

### 7.2 XP Curve

`xpToNext(L) = floor(85 * L^1.55)` — Level 10 at ~30 min, Level 30 at ~6 h, Level 60 at ~22 h, Level 100 at ~70 h. Creatures use `floor(65 * L^1.5)`, and reserve creatures gain 40% XP (no bench penalty spiral).

### 7.3 Gear

6 slots: Weapon, Offhand, Head, Body, Trinket ×2. Five tiers (Worn / Sound / Fine / Masterwork / Loomtouched). Gear has 1–3 affix slots; affixes are rolled from biome-weighted pools and re-rollable at the Forge. **No random legendary drops** — top-tier gear is always crafted from boss materials, so the reward loop is deterministic.

### 7.4 The Anchor (Base Building)

Grid-snapped, free placement within an Anchor Site's boundary (48×48 tiles at tier 1 → 128×128 at tier 5).

| Tier | Cost | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Starter | Storage 40 slots, campfire, bedroll |
| 2 | 200 timber, 80 iron | Forge, Kitchen, 2 Wardlights, creature pen (6) |
| 3 | Threadstone ×3 + Act II | Alchemy, Riftgate link, pen (12), farm plots |
| 4 | Boss material ×2 | Evolution Altar, auto-harvesters, pen (24) |
| 5 | Loom alloy ×5 | Cross-Shard teleport network, Loomforge, world-rule editor (post-Weave) |

Structures have **placement rules that matter**: Wardlights need line-of-sight coverage, farms need adjacent water, forges need ventilation. Building well is a puzzle; building badly still works, just worse.

## 8. Quests

### 8.1 Taxonomy

| Type | Count | Source | Authored? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Main (Threadline)** | 42 | Story beats | Fully authored |
| **Companion** | 9 × ~5 steps | Named NPCs | Fully authored |
| **Faction** | 4 × 8 | Faction hubs | Authored, order-flexible |
| **Bounty** | ∞ | Bounty Board | **Procedural** (§8.2) |
| **Discovery** | ~120 | World triggers | Authored one-liners |
| **Creature Requests** | ∞ | Bonded creatures | Procedural, bond-gated |

### 8.2 Procedural Quest Generator

Quests are assembled from a **grammar**, not a template soup:

```
QUEST   := GIVER + MOTIVE + OBJECTIVE{1..3} + REWARD + [TWIST]
MOTIVE  := survival | greed | grief | curiosity | duty | revenge
OBJECTIVE := FETCH(item, n, region)
           | HUNT(species|elite, n, region)
           | TAME(species, thread)
           | ESCORT(npc, from, to)
           | CLEAR(poi)
           | DELIVER(item, npc)
           | SURVIVE(duration, hazard)
           | BUILD(structure)
TWIST   := ambush | giver_lied | rival_claimant | creature_is_bonded_to_giver
```

Constraints enforced at generation: objective must be reachable with current unlocks; no FETCH for an item the player already holds ×5; reward scaled to `playerLevel` and travel distance; TWIST probability rises with faction Wary/Hostile standing. Quality gate — a generated quest is rejected and re-rolled if it duplicates any of the last 8 accepted quests on (objective type × region × species).

**Deliberate limit:** procedural quests never carry story. They carry *reasons to go somewhere you haven't been*, which is what an open world actually needs from them.

## 9. Economy

### 9.1 Currencies

| Currency | Source | Sink | Faucet/sink ratio target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Threadsilver** (soft) | Sales, bounties, caches | Vendors, respec, fast travel, repairs | 1.0 : 0.95 |
| **Relic Fragments** (mid) | Ruins, dungeons, bosses | Blueprints, evolution catalysts, affix rerolls | 1.0 : 1.1 (intentionally scarce) |
| **Loom Alloy** (endgame) | Bosses, Core | Tier-5 gear/base | Hard-capped, non-tradeable |
| **Faction Favor** (×4) | Faction quests | Faction vendors | Non-convertible |

**No premium currency. No IAP.** Premium price, full game.

### 9.2 Value Anchors

Base unit: 1 Threadsilver ≈ 1 second of low-risk activity.

| Item | Buy | Sell | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Timber ×10 | 20 | 8 | Sell price is always 40% of buy — prevents arbitrage loops |
| Iron Ingot | 45 | 18 | |
| Threadsnare | 60 | 24 | Primary early sink |
| Healing Salve | 80 | 32 | |
| Tier-3 Weapon | 2,400 | 960 | ≈ 40 min of play |
| Riftgate travel | 150/hop | — | Scales ×1.3 per Shard distance |
| Respec | 500 × (1 + respecCount × 0.25) | — | Escalating, resets each Act |

### 9.3 Inflation Control

1. **Vendor stock is finite and restocks on a 3-day cycle** — no infinite buy-low/sell-high.
2. **Repair costs scale with gear tier**, becoming the dominant late-game sink.
3. **Dynamic pricing:** selling >20 of an item to one vendor within a cycle drops its price 5% per unit, floored at 25%.
4. **Bounty rewards are computed, not fixed:** `reward = baseRate * difficulty * distance * repMultiplier`.

Target: player is *mildly* short of Threadsilver in Acts I–III, comfortable in Act IV, and spending on repairs/rerolls in Act V. Never rich enough to skip content, never poor enough to grind.

## 10. Combat

### 10.1 Model

**Real-time action combat with a tactical pause**, top-down, on the world map (no separate battle scene — key for mobile session flow and for making the open world feel dangerous).

- Player controls the Wayfinder directly. Three active creatures fight autonomously under a **Stance** the player sets (Aggressive / Balanced / Defensive / Hold) plus manual skill triggers.
- **Tactical Pause** (hold two-finger tap / spacebar): time slows to 15%, player queues one skill per creature. Not a full stop — pressure is preserved.
- Damage: `dmg = (ATK * skillPower / (1 + DEF/120)) * threadMult * critMult * randUniform(0.94, 1.06)`
  - `threadMult ∈ {0.67, 1.0, 1.5}` per §3.4; dual-thread multiplies.
  - `critMult = 1.75` at `critChance = 0.03 + Focus*0.004 + gear`.
  - Magic uses MAG/RES with the identical formula. One formula for everything — this is a balance decision, not laziness: two damage formulas is where every RPG's balance dies.
- **Stamina** gates player dodge/heavy attack; creatures use a per-skill cooldown, no mana. Fewer resources = readable on a 6" screen.

### 10.2 Player Skills

| Category | Examples | Resource |
|---|---|---|
| **Weapon arts** (per weapon class) | Cleave, Thrust-Through, Bowstorm, Guard-Break | Stamina |
| **Magic** (per Thread, 4 tiers) | Emberlance, Tidewall, Stonebind, Stormchain, Verdant Snare, Radiant Ward, Umbral Step | Focus meter (regen over time) |
| **Resonance** (bond ≥ 9) | Player + creature combo, one per Thread | Resonance charge (fills by dealing/taking damage) |

Weapon classes: Blade, Greatblade, Spear, Bow, Sling-Focus (magic), Fist-Claws. Each with a distinct 3-hit combo, a dodge-cancel window, and one art.

### 10.3 Status Effects

| Status | Effect | Stacks | Cured by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Burn** | 2% max HP/s, 6s | ×3 | Water, Tide skill |
| **Chill** | −15% SPD per stack | ×4 → Freeze | Ember, movement |
| **Freeze** | Stunned 2s, next hit ×1.5 | — | Any damage |
| **Poison** | 1.5% max HP/s, 12s, ignores DEF | ×5 | Antidote, Radiance |
| **Bleed** | 3% max HP per movement tick | ×3 | Bandage |
| **Shock** | 20% chance to interrupt skills | ×2 | Time |
| **Blind** | −40% accuracy | — | Radiance, time |
| **Confuse** | 25% chance to target random | — | Time, damage |
| **Root** | Cannot move, can act | — | Cleanse, time |
| **Weaken** | −25% ATK/MAG | ×2 | Cleanse |
| **Sunder** | −25% DEF/RES | ×2 | Cleanse |
| **Sanity Drain** | Deep-only; hallucinated enemies | — | Light, Radiance |

Rule: **no status can chain-lock a player.** Freeze and Root have a 6s per-target immunity after they expire. Enemies have no such protection — the player is allowed to be the one who cheats.

## 11. Crafting

### 11.1 Stations & Tiers

| Station | Tier | Produces |
|---|---|---|
| **Campfire** | 1 | Cooked food, torches, basic salves |
| **Workbench** | 1 | Tools, basic gear, building parts |
| **Forge** | 2 | Metal gear, ingots, repairs |
| **Kitchen** | 2 | Buff meals, creature-preferred food |
| **Alchemy Table** | 3 | Potions, antidotes, Threadsnares, catalysts |
| **Evolution Altar** | 4 | Evolution catalysts, Devolution Draughts |
| **Loomforge** | 5 | Loomtouched gear, world-rule shards |

### 11.2 Rules

- **Recipes are learned, not bought:** by discovery (harvest a new material → 60% chance to intuit a recipe), by blueprint, or by NPC teaching. This makes exploration feed crafting directly.
- **Quality roll:** `quality = clamp(0.5 + Craft*0.01 + stationTier*0.1 + rand(-0.1, 0.1))` → Crude/Standard/Fine/Superb, scaling stats ±20%.
- **Every crafted item is deconstructable** at 60% material return. No dead inventory.
- **Food matters:** meals grant timed buffs (+Warmth resist, +XP, +tame chance) and are the main use for farm output. Creature-preferred food is the fastest bond gain in the game.
- **No RNG failure.** Crafting never destroys materials. Quality varies; success does not.

## 12. Bosses

Eight total: six biome bosses (Shard-gating) and two world bosses (roaming, Act-triggered).

| # | Boss | Biome | Thread | Phases | Gimmick | Drops |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rootfather Ossuel** | Verdant Reach | Verdant | 2 | Spawns roots that must be cut to reach it; add pressure | Heartwood, Verdant catalyst |
| 2 | **The Kiln Widow** | Ashen Wastes | Ember | 3 | Arena floor heats in a spreading pattern; safe tiles rotate | Obsidian Heart, Ember catalyst |
| 3 | **Hoarking Vell** | Frostspire | Tide | 3 | Freezes party members; allies must break the ice | Rime Crystal, Tide catalyst |
| 4 | **Fenmother** | Sunken Mire | Verdant/Tide | 2 | Submerges; only trackable by ripples and creature Echolocate | Mire Pearl, Verdant catalyst |
| 5 | **Ozymand, the Buried Crown** | Dust Sea | Stone/Storm | 3 | Sandstorm phases blind the player; creature AI must carry | Relic Fragment ×5, Stone catalyst |
| 6 | **The Hollow Choir** | Hollow Deep | Umbra | 4 | Six bodies, one HP pool; killing the wrong one heals the rest | Threadstone Core, Umbra catalyst |
| 7 | **The Chorus of Rust** *(world)* | Roams | Storm/Null | 3 | Copies the player's own creature skills after seeing them | Loom Alloy ×2 |
| 8 | **The Loom Warden** *(final)* | Loom Core | Null | 5 | Each phase rewrites one game rule (gravity, thread chart, day/night) | Ending |

**Boss design rules:**
- Every boss is beatable at the intended level with *any* party composition; none require a specific Thread.
- Every boss telegraphs for ≥ 0.6s before a one-shot-class attack, and telegraphs are readable in colorblind-safe shape language, not color alone.
- Every boss has an **environmental answer** (Kiln Widow's floor can be cooled with Tide skills, etc.) that halves the fight for players who prepared.
- Boss HP scales with party size at 1.0/1.6/2.1 for 1/2/3 creatures — never linear, so soloing is hard but viable.

### 13.2 Seasonal / Live Events (post-launch, single-player)

Downloadable **Event Seeds** — small signed JSON bundles defining a themed Shard, a boss variant, cosmetic rewards, and a leaderboard-free challenge. They never touch balance in the main save, they run in a sandbox slot, and rewards are cosmetic + one catalyst. This is the entire live-ops surface for a premium single-player game, and it is deliberately small.

## 14. Accessibility

Non-negotiable, built in from day one:

- Full remapping (touch layout included — button positions are draggable and saved).
- Colorblind modes (protan/deutan/tritan) — **all** gameplay-critical info also encoded in shape or icon.
- Text scaling 80–200%, dyslexia-friendly font toggle, adjustable dialogue speed, no timed text.
- **Combat assists:** auto-target strength, damage reduction slider (25/50/75%), skip-boss-phase after 5 deaths, one-tap "auto-play creatures."
- Screen shake, flash, and particle-density sliders (photosensitivity: no full-screen flash > 3 Hz).
- Hold-to-press instead of tap-spam everywhere; no QTEs.
- Subtitles + captions for all audio cues, with directional indicators for off-screen threats.
- Survival meter drain rate: Normal / Relaxed / Off.

## 15. Art & Audio Direction (summary; full spec in AssetSpecifications.md)

- **Art:** Hand-painted 2D, 32×32 base tile, 48×48 to 96×96 characters/creatures, 8-directional (4 drawn + mirrored). Palette: 48-color master ramp, per-biome sub-ramps. Silhouette-first creature design — every creature must be identifiable as a black shape at 32px.
- **Lighting:** 2D normal maps + a light accumulation pass. Day/night is a graded LUT, not a color multiply.
- **Audio:** Adaptive vertical layering (exploration → tension → combat), per-biome instrument beds, dynamic Duskveil layer. Full mix under 60 MB via Ogg/Opus at 96 kbps mono for SFX, 128 kbps stereo for music.

## 16. Out of Scope for v1.0

Stated explicitly so it stays out: real-time multiplayer, PvP, player trading, guilds, voice acting, 3D, creature breeding, procedural creature generation, mod support, and any form of monetized live service. Architecture leaves seams for the first four (see TechnicalDesign §12) — nothing more.
